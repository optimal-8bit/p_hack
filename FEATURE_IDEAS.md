### 1. **Patient Health Dashboard** 🏥
**Description:** Comprehensive health tracking dashboard for patients
- **Features:**
  - Health metrics tracking (blood pressure, heart rate, weight, etc.)
  - Mood journal with calendar view
  - Symptom tracker with severity ratings
  - Medication adherence tracking
  - Lab results upload and history
  - Health goals and progress tracking
  - Integration with wearables (Fitwatch, Apple Health)
- **Why:** Gives patients a holistic view of their health journey
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Impact:** High - Better patient engagement
